# CHAPTER 1: Background & Purpose of the Scheme

### 1.1 Context and Problem Statement
* Under Section 403 of the Companies Act, 2013 (read with Companies Registration Rules, 2014), all registered companies must file their **Annual Returns** and **Financial Statements** annually.
* Since July 1, 2018, delayed filings incur a **mandatory additional fee of ₹100 per day**, without any upper cap.
* As India’s active company count has crossed 20 lakhs—including thousands of startups, MSMEs, Producer Companies, and One Person Companies (OPCs)—many entities accumulated massive financial burdens due to compounding daily default fees.

### 1.2 Objective of CCFS-2026
The Central Government, invoking powers under Section 460 read with Section 403 of the Companies Act, 2013, has launched the **CCFS-2026** as a **one-time amnesty scheme** to:
1. Allow non-compliant companies to clean up their backlog filings in the MCA-21 registry with **drastically reduced fee penalties**.
2. Update the corporate registry with accurate, real-time business data.
3. Provide a cheap, legal exit route for defunct/inactive companies via strike-off or obtaining "dormant" status.

# CHAPTER 2: The Three Business Options Under CCFS-2026

Businesses in default can choose one of three tracks during the scheme window (April 15, 2026 – July 15, 2026):

### 2.1 Option A: Complete Pending Filings (Regularize Business)
* **Who it is for:** Active businesses that want to continue operating legally but have missed annual filings.
* **Benefit:** Clear all pending forms by paying **only 10% of the calculated additional daily fee** (effectively a **90% discount** on penalties).

### 2.2 Option B: Apply for Dormant Company Status
* **Who it is for:** Inactive companies that are not currently running business operations but wish to retain their corporate entity/brand for future projects.
* **Benefit:** File **e-Form MSC-1** (under Section 455) at **50% of the normal filing fee**.

### 2.3 Option C: Company Closure / Strike-Off
* **Who it is for:** Inactive or defunct companies that wish to shut down permanently.
* **Benefit:** File **e-Form STK-2** for striking off the company name at **25% of the standard filing fee** (a **75% discount**).

# CHAPTER 3: Scope and Eligibility Criteria

### 3.1 Covered Forms (Relevant e-Forms)
The benefits of CCFS-2026 apply to delayed filings of the following e-forms:

#### Under Companies Act, 2013:
* **MGT-7 / MGT-7A:** Annual Return (for regular and small/OPC companies)
* **AOC-4 / AOC-4 CFS / AOC-4 XBRL / AOC-4 NBFC (Ind AS):** Financial Statements & Balance Sheets
* **ADT-1:** Appointment of Auditor
* **FC-3 / FC-4:** Filings by Foreign Companies

#### Under Companies Act, 1956 (Legacy Pending Filings):
* Form 20B, Form 21A, Form 23AC, Form 23ACA, Form 23AC-XBRL, Form 23ACA-XBRL, Form 66, and Form 23B.

### 3.2 Ineligible Companies (Exclusions)
The scheme **CANNOT** be used by:
1. Companies against which final strike-off notices under **Section 248** have already been initiated by the Registrar of Companies (ROC).
2. Companies that have already filed an application for striking off their name prior to the scheme.
3. Companies that already filed for **Dormant Status** (Section 455) before this scheme was announced.
4. Companies dissolved pursuant to a **scheme of amalgamation**.
5. **Vanishing Companies**.

# CHAPTER 5: Legal Immunity & Prosecution Relief

### 5.1 Relief for Annual Filings (Section 92 & Section 137 Defaults)
In terms of Section 454(3) of the Act, if pending filings are completed under CCFS-2026:
* **Full Immunity Given:** If filed **before** the Adjudicating Officer issues a notice, or **within 30 days** of receiving an adjudication notice. Proceedings under Section 92 (Annual Returns) and Section 137 (Financial Statements) will be closed without penal liability.
* **No Immunity Given:** If 30 days have already passed since the issuance of an adjudication notice, or if an Adjudication Order imposing penalties has already been passed. (The filing can still be made, but existing penalties remain payable).

### 5.2 Relief for Other Specific Forms (ADT-1, FC-3, FC-4, etc.)
Immunity against prospective penal action/prosecution for delay is granted **if**:
1. The forms are filed within the scheme duration (15.04.2026 to 15.07.2026).
2. No show-cause notice (SCN) or criminal prosecution has been initiated prior to the date of filing.

# CHAPTER 6: Post-Scheme Warnings & Action Plan

### 6.1 Regulatory Warning (Post-July 15, 2026)
Upon closure of the scheme on **July 15, 2026**, the Registrars of Companies (ROCs) are instructed to initiate strict legal action, penal proceedings, and full daily fee recoveries against all defaulting companies that failed to utilize this window.

### 6.2 Recommended Action Plan for Business Owners
1. **Compliance Audit:** Immediately review your company’s filing status on the MCA portal to identify unfiled forms (AOC-4, MGT-7, ADT-1, etc.).
2. **Calculate Savings:** Compute accrued ₹100/day fees versus the 10% discounted fee under the scheme.
3. **Decide Strategy:**
   * **Active Businesses:** Prepare financial statements and file delayed forms between April 15 and July 15, 2026.
   * **Inactive/Inoperative Businesses:** File **STK-2** (Strike-off) or **MSC-1** (Dormancy) to exit cleanly at 25% to 50% cost.
4. **Act Before April/May 2026:** Coordinate with your Company Secretary (CS) or Chartered Accountant (CA) to upload documents early in the scheme window to avoid last-minute portal slowdowns.
